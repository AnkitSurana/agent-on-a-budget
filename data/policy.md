# NovaMart Customer Policy

NovaMart is a pretend online shop used for this workshop.

## Refunds
- You can ask for a refund within **30 days of delivery**.
- The order must have the status **Delivered**.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method in **5–7 working days**.
- Refunds above **₹10,000** must be approved by a human agent.
- Track a refund any time by giving your order number.

## Cancellations
- An order can be cancelled for free while it is **Processing**.
- Once an order is **Shipped**, it cannot be cancelled. Wait for delivery and ask for a refund instead.
- There is **no cancellation fee**.

## Changing an order or address
- You can change the items or the delivery address only while the order is **Processing**.
- After the order ships, the address cannot be changed.

## Delivery
- Standard delivery: **3–5 working days**, free on orders above ₹499.
- Express delivery: **1–2 working days**, ₹99 extra.
- We deliver to all major cities in India.

## Payment methods
- UPI, credit cards, debit cards, net banking, and cash on delivery (orders up to ₹5,000).

## Invoices
- Invoices are emailed when the order ships.
- You can also download them from **My Orders** on the website.

## Account help
- Create an account or reset your password from the **Sign in** page.
- To delete an account, a human agent must confirm it.
- Newsletter: subscribe or unsubscribe from **Account → Settings → Emails**.

## Contact
- Chat with us 24/7 here, or email **support@novamart.example**.
- Ask for a human agent at any time.
